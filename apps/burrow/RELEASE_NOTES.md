Burrow writes up your talks on your own device: record or import a conversation and get a transcript with speakers, a summary with decisions and to-dos (with real due dates), the people in it, and answers to questions about it. Mask swaps personal details for placeholders before text goes to an AI.

- **iPhone and Mac** run the real on-device models: Desert Ant (Ear, Voz, Who, Gist, Redact, about 660 MB, offered on first start) and, if you add it on the models page, Gemma 4 E2B (2.6 GB; needs about 6 GB of RAM) for written summaries and ask Burrow. Without Gemma, notes are picked from the transcript by rules and say so.
- **Android** runs the same models; **Windows, Linux and the web** run demo engines with a sample transcript, labelled as demo.
- Nothing you record leaves the device. The only network traffic is model downloads, counted on the privacy page; "never go online" stops even those.

Not yet verified on a real iPhone or Mac: model downloads, recording permissions and inference were built in CI but not run on hardware. Please report what happens on the logs page (settings → logs → copy).
